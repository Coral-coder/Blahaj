How to play

The meter at the top is Leo’s dream. It slowly fades while he’s alone, and it drains fast in the gloom that pools round each nightmare on the floor. Poof every one of them — jump on them, belly flop or dash through — and the gloom goes with them (the way out stays shut until they’re all gone). Pools of light and furniture are always safe. Collect 🐟 fish, switch on lamps, and find the three hidden ⭐ starfish in every chapter to keep the dream sweet. The 🐰 dust bunnies are friends: land on one for a big, giggly bounce. Jump on nightmares to make them go poof.
