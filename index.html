Blåhaj's
Big Adventure

A soft and squishy 3D platformer about a very good shark.
Press M to toggle sound · works with keyboard, gamepad and touch

How to play

WASD / arrows: swim
Space: jump (hold for higher)
Space in the air: 🫧 double jump
C in the air: 💥 belly flop
Shift: 🚀 torpedo dash
Hold Space falling: 🪽 fin glide
Drag mouse or Q/E: turn camera
Esc pause · R restart

Collect 🐟 fish and find the three ⭐ starfish hidden in every level. Bop 🐰 dust bunnies by landing on them, belly flop the robo-vacuums, and snuggle the big pillow to finish. Each level you finish teaches Blåhaj a new trick, and enough starfish open a secret bonus level.
